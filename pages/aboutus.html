About Us

Our bowling alley began as a small family dream to create a place where people could come together and enjoy simple, meaningful moments. Starting from a single idea it grew quickly into a space filled with laughter, friendly competition, and community, spirit.

1970s - The Start of the Bowling Alley
This bowling alley began as a small, family-driven dream in the 1970s. With just a few lanes and a simple setup, it became a place where friends and family would gather to enjoy their time together. From the start, the main goal was to create a space where the community could have fun.
Early 2000s
As the years went on, the bowling alley grew alongside the community. New features, updated lanes, and a more modern atmosphere were introduced to the bowling alley to enhance the experience.
Currently
Today, our bowling alley is a vibrant and welcoming space for all ages, where people can come together to celebrate and enjoy time with friends and family. With the modern lanes, delicious food, and welcomin atmosphere, it's a great spot for both special events and causual outings.
